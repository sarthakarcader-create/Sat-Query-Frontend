SAT QUERY AI

About Us

We built SatQuery AI, an agentic vision-language platform that makes satellite imagery accessible without requiring specialized remote-sensing expertise. It reasons over optical and radar data such as Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 to answer natural-language questions and extract meaningful insights from Earth observation imagery, enabling applications across disaster management, urban planning, agriculture, and environmental monitoring.

Technology

We use an agentic architecture that interprets each query and dynamically selects the appropriate specialist model instead of relying on a single VLM. This enables task-specific analysis across VQA, captioning, change detection, and image fusion.

We fuse optical and SAR imagery to combine spectral information with radar-based observations. Bi-temporal analysis enables us to identify and localize changes between satellite images captured at different times.

We preserve native satellite data by processing multi-band GeoTIFFs directly through Rasterio/GDAL rather than converting them to simplified RGB images. Metadata validation and spatial co-registration ensure the imagery remains accurately aligned.

We maintain an auditable inference pipeline from query parsing to model execution and final output, with visual evidence and execution traces. Large raster data is separated from lightweight Parquet annotations to support efficient query processing.

Services

Bi-Temporal Change Analysis

Analyze spatially corresponding images acquired at different times for change detection, change description, and Change-VQA.

Optical–SAR Analysis

Combines co-registered optical/multispectral and Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery of the same geographic area to extract complementary information from both sources, enabling more comprehensive analysis than either image alone.

Visual Question Answering (VQA)

Enables users to ask natural-language questions about satellite imagery and receive relevant answers based on the land cover, objects, and scene conditions present in the image.
